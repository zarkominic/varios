## Descripción (EN)

On a hillside in eastern Turkey there is a rock shaped like a ship. Some say it is Noah's Ark; geologists say it is just a
rock. In 2026 a team drilled into it and announced charred wood. Nothing has been confirmed. This is the story of the
flood, the religions that tell it, the clay tablets older than the Bible, and what has really been found.

What we cover:
- 1959: a Turkish army captain, an aerial photo and a ship-shaped outline near Mount Ararat;
- the story in Genesis, and the one in the Quran, where the ark rests on a different mountain;
- London, 1872: George Smith reads the flood on a clay tablet from the Epic of Gilgamesh;
- the round ark of a 3,700-year-old tablet, and the floods of India and Greece;
- why so many peoples tell it: rivers, mud layers and a disputed Black Sea hypothesis;
- Ron Wyatt, David Fasold and the 1996 paper that called it a natural formation;
- 2026: radar, soil samples, drilling and charred wood, and why none of it is confirmed yet;
- what it would take to confirm it.
