## Descripción (EN)

A map from 1513 seems to show Antarctica, three hundred years before anyone saw it. For decades it has been called
proof of a lost civilization, and even of aliens. But the real story is stranger: hidden inside it is a lost map by
Christopher Columbus.

What we cover:
- 1929: a folded gazelle-skin parchment found in Istanbul's Topkapı Palace;
- Piri Reis, his corsair uncle Kemal Reis, and a sailor captured off Spain who had crossed the ocean with Columbus;
- the twenty charts Piri Reis combined, and why Hispaniola is drawn like Japan;
- Cabral, Brazil, and the legend of Saint Brendan's giant fish;
- Charles Hapgood, the 1960 Air Force letter, and Maps of the Ancient Sea Kings;
- what the ice, the Drake Passage and the edge of the parchment really tell us;
- pareidolia: why your brain sees things on a map that aren't there.
