## Descripción (EN)

In March 2025, three Italian researchers said that under the second pyramid of Giza, eight giant shafts sink more than
six hundred meters into the earth. Nobody has proven it yet. But Giza does hide real secrets, and some were found very
recently.

What we cover:
- what Malanga, Biondi and Mei say is under Khafre: five chambers, eight spiral shafts, two giant cubes;
- how they say they saw it: satellite radar and the tiny vibrations of the pyramid;
- what really was found: the Big Void (2017) and the hidden corridor (2023), with muons and a camera thinner than a pencil;
- the Osiris Shaft and the L-shaped anomaly of 2024;
- what the experts say, the groundwater, and the 2026 retraction;
- the Labyrinth that Herodotus said surpassed the pyramids;
- why "not proven" doesn't mean "a lie".
