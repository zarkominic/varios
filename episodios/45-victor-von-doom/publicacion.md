Before he was Marvel's most feared villain, Victor von Doom was a little boy in the snow who wanted to save his mother.
This is his whole life in the comics, told from inside the iron mask: the night his father gave him his coat, the machine
that scarred him, the rival he could never forgive, the mother he finally freed, and how he ended up as the king of hell.

⚠️ Spoilers: his whole story in the comics, up to October 2026.

  Mephisto;
