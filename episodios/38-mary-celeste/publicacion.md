In December 1872, a group of sailors found a ship drifting alone in the middle of the Atlantic. There was no one on
board. The cargo was intact, there was food for six months, and the ten people who had left New York on it were never
seen again. This is the story of a ship that changed her name, a captain who brought his family along, a prosecutor who
saw blood everywhere, a young Arthur Conan Doyle who embellished the legend, and the chemists who blew up a model ship
in 2026 to understand that morning.
