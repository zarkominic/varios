Before Katniss, another kid from District 12 beat the Capitol at its own Games, and the Capitol made him pay for it. That kid is Haymitch, and his story hits theaters on November 20 in The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping. Here's what to remember, who's who, and the clues the earlier movies left for you.

What we cover:
- the essentials: Panem, the tributes, Katniss and her mentor;
- the timeline: the 10th, 50th and 74th Games, and why the saga runs backwards;
- Haymitch's Games: the second Quarter Quell, 48 tributes, and an ending the books already told;
- who's who: Joseph Zada, Whitney Peak, Mckenna Grace, Ralph Fiennes, Jesse Plemons, Elle Fanning, Kieran Culkin, Glenn Close, and who's coming back;
- the clues: the mockingjay pin, a last name, a line in Catching Fire, and Wiress's clock;
- what's real behind Panem: bread and circuses, Theseus, David Hume, the real mockingbird, and the three-finger salute;
- what to watch first if you're short on time.

No spoilers from the new book: only the official premise, the cast, the trailer and what the original trilogy already revealed.
