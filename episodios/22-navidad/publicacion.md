## Descripción (EN)

Christmas was once illegal. In 1647, England's Parliament abolished it, and in Massachusetts you could be fined five
shillings for celebrating. So how much of what we believe about Christmas is actually true? We check the myths
everyone repeats, then travel around the world, following the calendar, to see how differently it's celebrated.

What we cover:
- when Christmas was banned: the Puritans, the Canterbury riot, and the Massachusetts fine;
- Santa Claus: Saint Nicholas, the 1823 poem, Thomas Nast, and why Coca-Cola didn't make him red;
- why December 25: the Roman calendar of 354, two theories, and the Saturnalia;
- the tree, the mistletoe, Jingle Bells in space, and the X in Xmas;
- around the world: the Philippines, Gävle's straw goat, Krampus, Iceland's Yule Lads, the posadas and the
  poinsettia, Catalonia's Tió, KFC in Japan, Australia's beach Christmas and Italy's Befana;
- the 1914 Christmas truce.
