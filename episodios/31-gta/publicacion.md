## Descripción (EN)

The video game that, so far, has made a billion dollars faster than any other was born in Scotland, at the studio behind a
game about tiny creatures, and it was almost canceled. This is the story of Grand Theft Auto, just before GTA VI.

What we cover:
- Dundee, DMA Design and the little creatures that became a worldwide hit in 1991;
- Race'n'Chase (1995): cops and robbers, and the button for switching cars;
- the police that "went crazy", according to Gary Penn, and the publisher that wanted to cancel it;
- Max Clifford, the House of Lords and the tabloid storm of 1997;
- Take-Two, the Houser brothers and the birth of Rockstar Games;
- Grand Theft Auto III, September 11th and the jump to 3D;
- Hot Coffee, Jack Thompson and the records of Grand Theft Auto V;
- the 2022 leak, the most-watched trailer and the wait for November 19.
