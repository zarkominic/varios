## Descripción (EN)

In August 1590, John White finally made it back to his colony in America. He had left more than a hundred people there,
among them his daughter and his three-year-old granddaughter. No one was left. All he found was one word carved into a
post: "CROATOAN." This is the story of a painter who ended up as a governor, the first English child born in the
Americas, a fleet that delayed everything, trees that remember the weather of those years, a map with a secret under a
patch, and archaeologists who search with magnets.

What we cover:
- Walter Raleigh, Queen Elizabeth and the 1584 voyage to the Outer Banks;
- Manteo and Wanchese, and the Algonquian peoples of Roanoke and Croatoan;
- the soldiers of 1585, John White's watercolors, and why it went wrong;
- 1587: about 115 settlers, families instead of soldiers, and the pilot who wouldn't go on;
- Virginia Dare, born on August 18, 1587;
- the signal they agreed on: a name, and a cross if they left in danger;
- the Spanish Armada, French pirates and three lost years;
- 1590: "CRO" on a tree, "CROATOAN" on a post, and no cross;
- the 1587–1589 drought in the cypress rings;
- the patch on White's map at the British Museum, and Site X;
- Hatteras: a sword hilt, a slate, a ring, and the iron flakes announced in 2025;
- John Lawson's 1709 account, the doubts, the rumors and the fake stones of 1937.
